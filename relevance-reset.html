What This Pathway Is About

Relevance Reset™ supports adults who have begun to question whether their experience, knowledge and contribution still matter.

Sometimes, the world around us changes faster than our sense of where we fit within it. Work may be changing. Retirement may have created unexpected space. Technology may feel as though it is moving ahead without us. Family roles may have shifted, and the visibility or recognition we once had may no longer be there.

These changes can quietly raise an uncomfortable question.

Do I still have something valuable to offer?

Relevance Reset™ gives you space to explore that question.

The pathway helps you look at your experience with fresh eyes and recognise what you already carry. Years of work, life experience, relationships, challenges and personal growth can develop knowledge and strengths that are easy to overlook, particularly when a familiar role or position has changed.

Your value does not disappear simply because the role that once made it visible has changed.

Relevance Reset™ helps you explore where the sense of irrelevance may have begun and separate visibility, employment and position from deeper value. It also creates space to identify what may need updating and consider how your experience can be translated into meaningful contribution in the present.

This is not about competing with anyone younger or trying to prove your worth.

It is about recognising what you already carry, remaining open to growth and change, and exploring where your knowledge, skills, experience and perspective may still be needed.

Three Pathway Depths

You can enter Relevance Reset™ at one of three depths. The right level depends on how much space you need, how complex your questions feel, and whether you are seeking greater clarity or want support putting new choices into practice.

The aim is not simply to look back at what you have done.

It is to help you recognise what remains valuable, understand what may need to change, and explore where you can continue to make a meaningful contribution.

Relevance Reset™ is the second stage of the Purpose2Destiny CLL Transformation Framework™. Building on the clarity developed through Identity Reset™, it helps you move from understanding who you are now to recognising where you still matter and how your experience can continue to have meaning and value.

What You May Gain

  • A stronger understanding of the value you still carry
  • Greater confidence in your transferable strengths
  • A clearer view of where your experience may be useful now
  • A realistic contribution or learning step to test

The Purpose2Destiny Approach

Journey · Session Flow

REFLECT

We begin with what is happening now. You will have space to describe the issue in your own words and notice what may be underneath it.

REALIGN

We identify what matters most, what still fits, and what may need to change or be viewed differently.

ACTIVATE

You leave with one realistic next step, reflection or small experiment rather than a long list of things to do.
