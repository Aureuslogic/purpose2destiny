Privacy Policy

Purpose2Destiny is committed to protecting your privacy and treating your personal information with fairness, transparency and respect. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how long we keep it, who we may share it with, and the rights available to you.

It applies whenever you visit our website, contact us, purchase a product or service, register for a programme, subscribe to our communications or otherwise engage with Purpose2Destiny.

This Privacy Policy relates only to the handling of personal information. The contractual terms governing our coaching services, workshops, programmes and digital products are contained within our Terms of Service.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, billing details and information you voluntarily provide through enquiries, bookings, coaching applications, programme registrations, downloads or newsletters.

Information and Marketing Choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly. Service-related communications relating to bookings or purchases will continue where necessary.

Order Forms and Purchases

Information collected during purchases is used solely to process payments, deliver services, maintain business records and provide customer support. Payments are processed securely through trusted third-party providers.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, analyse website performance and remember user preferences. You can manage cookie settings through your browser.

Sharing Information

We never sell personal information. We share personal data only with trusted providers who support payment processing, website hosting, scheduling, email communications or similar business operations.

GDPR and Privacy Rights

Where applicable, you may request access to your personal information, request correction or deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, withdraw consent or request data portability.

Confidentiality

Information shared with us is treated confidentially except where disclosure is required by law or where there is a genuine safeguarding concern involving serious harm.

Responsible AI

Artificial intelligence may assist with administration or content preparation. Human oversight remains central and client confidentiality is protected.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The most recent version will always be published on our website.
