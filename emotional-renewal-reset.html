Who Are We?

We are experienced coaches who support adults as they navigate change, reflect on their experiences, and make intentional choices about what comes next.

We do not diagnose, treat or provide therapy. Instead, we create a compassionate, supportive coaching space where you can explore what may be influencing you, gain greater clarity about your thoughts, beliefs and choices, and consider practical ways to move forward.

Our work is grounded in self-awareness, personal responsibility and purposeful action. We believe that meaningful change begins with recognising where you are, understanding what may be shaping your choices, and deciding what you want to do next.

How Can We Help You?

We help you recognise what may be influencing you, understand patterns, beliefs and experiences that may be affecting your present choices, and explore practical ways to move forward with greater clarity and intention.

We create space for you to reflect on grief, regret, self doubt and significant life experiences without turning coaching into therapy. Our focus is on what you can understand, what you can choose, and what you want to do differently as you move into your next chapter.

As qualified lawyers, certified coaches, NLP practitioners and holders of a Diploma in Counselling, we bring a combination of professional training, practical experience and human understanding to our coaching.

We create a supportive space free from judgement, pressure and unrealistic expectations, while remaining clear about the boundaries of coaching and when specialist support may be more appropriate.

What This Pathway Is About

Emotional Renewal Reset™ supports adults who recognise that past experiences, emotions or patterns may still be influencing how they think, respond and make choices today.

Life can leave us carrying disappointment, loss, regret, responsibility and experiences that have shaped the way we see ourselves and the world around us. Often, we simply keep going. We meet our responsibilities, care for others, build careers, manage change and continue with life.

Then there may come a point when you begin to notice that something from the past is affecting a choice you are making today.

You may find yourself asking:

What am I ready to acknowledge, understand and release?

Emotional Renewal Reset™ provides a structured coaching space to step back, reflect and gain perspective. The focus is on recognising what you are experiencing now, understanding the patterns or responses that may have developed, and considering the choices available to you today.

You cannot change what happened, but you can become more intentional about what you choose next.

Coaching does not require you to revisit every painful experience or disclose anything you do not wish to discuss. Instead, the work focuses on what is relevant to you now and what you want to do with that awareness.

You may begin to recognise that a past experience has influenced a current decision. A disappointment may have affected how willing you are to trust again. A long-standing responsibility may have made it difficult to establish boundaries. A familiar way of responding may no longer reflect the person you are becoming.

Through coaching, you can step back from these patterns, explore your perspective and consider what you want to do differently.

The pathway is built around three movements:

Recognise. Understand. Choose.

Recognise what you are noticing in yourself and what may be influencing your current choices.

Understand the patterns, beliefs, responses and circumstances that may have shaped your perspective.

Choose what you want to do with that understanding. This may include setting a boundary, changing a response, seeking appropriate support, making a different decision or taking a practical step forward.

The emphasis is on choice and forward movement, not treatment.

Emotional Renewal Reset™ is a coaching pathway. It is not counselling, psychotherapy, medical care or crisis support. It does not diagnose, treat or claim to heal trauma, grief, mental health conditions or significant psychological distress. Where specialist support is appropriate, you may be encouraged to seek help from a suitably qualified professional.

Three Pathway Depths

You can enter Emotional Renewal Reset™ at one of three depths. The right level depends on how much space you need, how complex the question feels, and whether you are seeking clarity alone or want time and structure to put new choices into practice.

The aim is not to change your past or provide therapeutic treatment.

It is to create a coaching space where you can recognise what is present, understand what may be influencing you, and choose how you want to respond as you move forward.

Emotional Renewal Reset™ is the third stage of the Purpose2Destiny Transformation Framework™. Building on the identity clarity developed through Identity Reset™ and the sense of contribution explored through Relevance Reset™, this stage creates space to recognise what may still influence the present, understand it with greater clarity, and make intentional choices about the next chapter of your life.

What You May Gain

  • Greater awareness of what has been affecting you emotionally.
  • A less judgemental understanding of recurring patterns.
  • Clearer boundaries and support needs.
  • A kinder and more realistic way of responding to yourself.
  • Greater clarity about what belongs within coaching and when counselling, medical or other professional support may be more appropriate.

The Purpose2Destiny Approach

Journey · Session Flow

REFLECT

REALIGN
