Who are we?

We help capable, thoughtful adults make sense of who they are now: not who they used to be, not who they were expected to be. We are not here to reinvent you or push you into a new identity. We help you slow down, listen, and reconnect with the parts of yourself that went quiet while you were busy being responsible.

How can we help you?

We help you untangle the confusion you have been carrying quietly, understand why you feel restless, flat, or disconnected, and rebuild trust in your own voice and inner sense of direction. You do not leave with motivation. You leave with clarity, steadiness, and self-trust.

Why should you trust us?

We do not rush you, fix you, or talk over your lived experience. We have worked with people at this exact stage of life: professionals, parents, leaders: who felt lost in ways they could not explain. We meet you where you are, we respect your pace, and we do not pretend this season is simple.

Why should you hire us?

Because we respect your life experience and do not treat you like a beginner or a problem to fix. Because we do not rush you or push you: purpose is not downloaded, it is uncovered. Because we know how to guide people through change gently and clearly.

What This Pathway Is About

Identity Reset™ supports adults who feel that an older version of themselves no longer fully fits.

Sometimes, life changes before we have had time to understand what those changes mean for who we are. A career may be ending or changing. Children may need us differently. A relationship may have changed. Retirement, relocation, loss, personal growth, or simply the passing of time can leave us quietly asking, “Who am I now?”

Identity Reset™ gives you space to explore that question.

The pathway helps you understand the identity story you have been living and recognise how your roles, responsibilities, relationships and life experiences have shaped the way you see yourself. It also helps you notice where change, loss, growth or disconnection may have created a gap between who you have been and who you are becoming.

Sometimes the question is not, “What should I do next?” It is, “Who am I becoming?”

You may have spent years being a parent, partner, professional, provider, carer, leader or the person everyone depended on. These roles can become so familiar that it is difficult to separate who you are from what you do for others.

When those roles change, identity can feel uncertain too.

Identity Reset™ creates space to reconnect with the person underneath the responsibilities and expectations. It is not about rejecting your past or becoming someone completely different. It is about understanding your story, recognising what has changed, and developing a clearer sense of who you are now.

Three Pathway Depths

You can enter Identity Reset™ at one of three depths. The right level depends on how much space you need, how complex your questions feel, and whether you are seeking greater clarity or want support putting new choices into practice.

The aim is not to rush you towards an answer.

It is to help you understand where you are now, what may have changed, and what your next chapter could look like.

Identity Reset™ is the first stage of the Purpose2Destiny CLL Transformation Framework™. It creates the foundation for the stages that follow by helping you become clearer about who you are before exploring where you still matter, what may need emotional renewal, and how you can activate your purpose.

What You May Gain

  • Greater clarity about who you are beyond your main roles.
  • A stronger understanding of what matters to you now.
  • Permission to evolve without dismissing the life you have already lived.
  • Clearer boundaries and choices that reflect your present values.
  • A practical way to give more room to parts of yourself that have been overlooked.

The Purpose2Destiny Approach

Journey · Session Flow

REFLECT

We begin with what is happening now. You will have space to describe the issue in your own words and notice what may be underneath it.

REALIGN

We identify what matters most, what still fits, and what may need to change or be viewed differently.

ACTIVATE

You leave with one realistic next step, reflection or small experiment rather than a long list of things to do.

Which Depth May Be Right for Me?

Depth May suit you if Main emphasis
